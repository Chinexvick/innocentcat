 1. Executive Summary
 Innocent Cat ($ICAT) is a community-oriented crypto token built around a simple identity:
 innocence, compassion, community, and the belief that a digital community can eventually create
 positive real-world impact.

 The project combines the cultural and community appeal of a cat-themed token with a longer-term
 social-impact vision. A central part of that vision is charity, particularly support for orphanages and
 people who need practical assistance.

 The project's stated total supply is 100,000,000 ICAT, with a 3% buy tax and 3% sell tax. The
 contract address supplied for the project is listed in Section 10.

 Innocent Cat is designed to grow through community participation, transparent communication,
 responsible development, and the gradual expansion of its social-impact ambitions.


 2. The Innocent Cat Story
 Innocent Cat is inspired by a personal story of resilience. The project's origin story centers on a
 developer who grew up without a father or mother and chose to build something that could
 eventually become a vehicle for helping others.

 The name Innocent Cat represents more than a visual brand. It is intended to symbolize
 vulnerability, simplicity, kindness, and the idea that people who have experienced hardship can
 still create something meaningful for others.

 The project's long-term charitable direction includes supporting orphanages and helping
 vulnerable people. The intention is to make social impact part of the project's identity rather than
 treating charity as a short-term promotional campaign.

            “From an innocent beginning to a community with a purpose.”

 3. Mission & Vision
 Mission: To build a recognizable and engaged digital community around Innocent Cat while
 creating a credible path toward real-world charitable impact.

 Vision: To develop Innocent Cat into a community-led ecosystem where the token, its brand, and
 its future initiatives can connect people, creativity, and charitable action.

 The project is intentionally designed around a long-term mindset. Growth is expected to come
 from community building, responsible development, partnerships, visibility, and useful initiatives
 rather than promises of guaranteed returns.


 4. The Problem
 Many meme and community tokens are built primarily around attention and short-term
 speculation. While community culture can be powerful, a project can struggle to maintain a
 meaningful identity if there is no broader purpose.

 At the same time, many charitable causes lack consistent visibility, funding, and community
 participation. Orphanages and vulnerable families often need practical support that goes beyond
 awareness.

 5. The Innocent Cat Approach
 Community first: Build an active, respectful, and engaged community around the Innocent Cat
 identity.

 Transparency: Communicate token parameters, project developments, and major decisions
 clearly.

 Long-term development: Focus on sustainable initiatives rather than relying solely on short-term
 market hype.

 Social impact: Develop practical charity initiatives as resources and operational capacity grow.

 Responsible communication: Avoid guaranteed-return claims and encourage users to make
 independent decisions.


 6. Charity & Social Impact Vision
 Charity is one of the defining long-term goals of Innocent Cat. The project's initial social-impact
 direction is focused on orphanages and vulnerable people.

 Potential future initiatives may include donations to orphanages, provision of food and essential
 supplies, support for education, medical assistance, and other practical forms of help. Specific
 programs, beneficiaries, budgets, and schedules will depend on available resources and future
 project development.

 The project will aim to make charitable activities visible and accountable through appropriate
 records, community communication, and evidence of completed initiatives where privacy and
 safeguarding considerations permit.

 Important: No fixed percentage of token tax is assigned to charity in the information currently
 provided for this white paper. Future charity funding should therefore be treated as a project
 objective, not as an automatic contractual entitlement of token holders.

 9. Tax Structure
 Innocent Cat currently specifies a 3% buy tax and 3% sell tax.

 A transaction tax can be used by a token's smart contract to fund designated ecosystem activities,
 but the exact destination of the tax must be defined by the contract and verified on-chain.
 Because no tax allocation breakdown was supplied, this white paper does not claim that a
 particular percentage goes to liquidity, marketing, development, charity, or another wallet.

 The project's commitment is to communicate any confirmed tax allocation clearly and to avoid
 presenting unverified wallet usage as fact.
