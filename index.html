How to buy

Innocent Cat is a community token inspired by a story of resilience: a developer who grew up without a father or mother and chose to build something that could one day help others. The name stands for vulnerability, simplicity and kindness, and for the belief that people who have known hardship can still create something meaningful. Our long-term mission is real-world charity, starting with orphanages and vulnerable people. From an innocent beginning to a community with a purpose.

Create a BSC wallet

Download MetaMask, Trust Wallet or the wallet of your choice from the App Store or Google Play for free. Desktop users can add the browser extension.

Get some BNB

Have BNB in your wallet to swap for $ICAT. If you don't have any, buy BNB on an exchange and send it to your wallet.

How to buy $ICAT?

Open PancakeSwap in your wallet's browser, connect your wallet and paste the official contract address. Enter the amount of BNB you want to swap and confirm. $ICAT has a 3% buy tax, so set slippage slightly above 3%.

Verify the contract

Only use the official address below, also pinned in our Telegram. Check it on the block explorer before you buy.
