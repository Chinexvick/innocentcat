Chapter one · The Innocent Cat story

Born from resilience

Innocent Cat is inspired by a personal story. Its developer grew up without a father or mother and chose to build something that could one day become a way of helping others.

The name stands for vulnerability, simplicity and kindness, and for the idea that people who have known hardship can still create something meaningful for others. Charity is part of who we are, not a short-term campaign.

“From an innocent beginning to a community with a purpose.”

Our mission

To build a recognizable, engaged community around Innocent Cat while creating a credible path toward real-world charitable impact.

Our vision

A community-led ecosystem where the token, the brand and future initiatives connect people, creativity and charitable action.

Charity & social impact

Orphanages and vulnerable people at the centre

Charity is one of the defining long-term goals of Innocent Cat. As the community grows, we aim to turn that growth into visible, accountable help, shared with records and proof of completed initiatives where privacy and safeguarding allow.

  • Food and essential supplies
  • Donations to orphanages
  • Support for education
  • Medical assistance
  • Other practical help

No fixed share of the token tax is assigned to charity yet. Future charity funding is a project goal, not an automatic entitlement of holders.
